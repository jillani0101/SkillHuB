Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Information we collect

When you register, we collect your name (or username), email address, and a securely hashed password. You may optionally add a bio and the skills you want to show on your profile. If you sign in with Google, we use your name, email, and Google account ID.

How we use it

We use your information to run the site: authenticating you, letting you apply to and manage projects, sending notification and account emails, and keeping the community safe. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Emails

We only email you for account-related reasons: verification, password resets, application updates, and (if you contact us) replies to your messages. There is no marketing or newsletter.

Data storage & security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never readable in plain text. We use HTTPS on the live site, secure cookies, and industry-standard protections. No system is completely immune to attack, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Your choices

You can edit your profile or password at any time from your Settings page. To delete your account or remove your data, contact us and we’ll do it for you.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch.

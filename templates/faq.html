Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I create an account?

Click Sign Up Free, enter your details, and confirm your email address using the link we send you. You can also sign in with Google.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Verification confirms you own the email address you signed up with. It keeps the community real and lets you recover your account if you ever forget your password.

How do I apply to a project?

Open the project page and click Apply to Join. The owner reviews applications from their Requests page and can accept or reject each one.

How do I find teammates for my own project?

Create a project, list the skills it needs, and applicants will come to you. You can also join other projects as a member.

How does the team chat work?

Once you’re an accepted member, you can open the project’s chat from your Team page or directly from the project card and talk to your whole team in real time.

What if I forget my password?

Use the Forgot password link on the login page. We’ll email you a link to set a new one.

Can I delete my project?

Yes — project owners can delete their project from the edit screen. Projects can also be paused or marked completed at any time.

Still stuck?

Reach out through our contact page and we’ll help you out.
